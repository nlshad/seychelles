Streamlined Workflow

How Your Cargo Travels With Us

A transparent, dependable 4-step logistics journey from initial pickup to doorstep delivery.

01

Collection & Warehouse Receiving

Cargo is collected from your supplier or dropped off at our Bur Dubai or China logistics hub. All packages are weighed, measured, barcoded, and inspected.

02

Packing & CBM Consolidation

Items are repackaged, shrink-wrapped, or crated in heavy-duty wooden boxes. LCL cargo is grouped securely into ocean containers for maximum cost efficiency.

03

Ocean Sailing / Express Flight

Your cargo departs on scheduled weekly vessels from Jebel Ali or daily flights from DXB. You receive sailing schedules, ETA updates, and tracking confirmation.

04

Customs Clearance & Delivery

Upon arrival at Port Victoria, Port Louis, or destination ports, our team handles customs clearance, duty payment, container deconsolidation, and direct delivery.
