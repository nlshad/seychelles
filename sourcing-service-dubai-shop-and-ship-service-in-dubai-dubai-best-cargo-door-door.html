Shop in Dubai & Ship Directly to Seychelles

Our Shop & Ship / Sourcing Service allows clients in Seychelles, Mauritius, Zanzibar, and Africa to purchase goods, electronics, building materials, clothing, or auto parts from Dubai & China suppliers and ship them easily to their home country.

How Shop & Ship Works:

  1. Purchase or Request Sourcing: Buy online or ask us to source items from Dubai wholesale markets (Dragon Mart, Deira, Bur Dubai).
  2. Deliver to Our Dubai Warehouse: Have your packages delivered to our secure Dubai warehouse address.
  3. Inspection & Consolidation: We combine multiple packages into one box/pallet to save you shipping costs.
  4. Dispatch & Delivery: We ship your cargo via Air or Sea to your home address or destination port in Seychelles.
